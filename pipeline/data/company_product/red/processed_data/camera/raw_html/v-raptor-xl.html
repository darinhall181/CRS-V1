System

          
The XL System features an all new three-stage cooling system. Dedicated fans cool both auxiliary power components and camera components separately, while a thermoelectric heat exchanger maintains precise sensor temperature. This advanced cooling system means quieter operation, less black shading, and a more robust system no matter the environment.

WATER AND DUST RESISTANT

        
*V-RAPTOR is manufactured with water and dust-resistant sealing materials in critical areas to mitigate damage and provide a rugged build that is suitable for a variety of environmental situations. The camera is NOT water-proof or dust-proof, and standard precautions in handling high-end electronics should still be taken in all environmental situations. Do not place or submerge the camera in any liquid. RED Digital Cinema retains the right to investigate any and all damage as it pertains to warranty coverage.
