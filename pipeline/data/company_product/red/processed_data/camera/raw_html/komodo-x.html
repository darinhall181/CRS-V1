FEATURES & BENEFITS

        
The next evolution of KOMODO features all-new sensor technology that multiplies frame rate and dynamic range performance within a new advanced platform and all-new Z Mount option for ultimate lens flexibility.

    NEXT GENERATION GLOBAL SHUTTER SENSOR
    
            
    KOMODO-X features a completely overhauled sensor architecture, with updated pixel design and improvements in both detail and color in shadows.
    
          
    • 
          
  • 
            
    Improved Body Design
    
            
    A robust locking lens mount, with professional Micro V-Lock battery interface and all-new assignable tactile buttons.
    
          
    • 
        

      

      

        
    
          
  • 
            
    6K AT 80 FPS & 4K AT 120 FPS
    
            
    KOMODO-X achieves 80 fps in 6K and 120 fps in 4K, thanks to the new sensor design and processing, as well as support for CFexpress.
    
          
    • 
          
  • 
            
    ADVANCED PLATFORM
    
            
    Expanded I/O, including updated monitoring capabilities with support for DSMC3 Touch LCD, built-in USB Type-C, and newly engineered audio hardware, including locking +48V audio connector with improved preamp.

INTERFACE EXPANSION

        
KOMODO-X Offers robust and powerful systems, with USB Type-C interfaces, ethernet offload capabilities, Timecode and Genlock compatibility and more.

      

      

        
          
          
        
        

          

            
TOUCH SCREEN DISPLAY

            
Integrated onboard LCD provides comprehensive control and image preview for smallest possible configuration.

          

          

            
DSMC3 TOUCH MONITOR SUPPORT

            
Monitor and control the camera using the direct attach 7” DSMC3 Touch LCD, leaving the SDI available for wireless transmitters or other monitors.

          

          

            

            
TACTILE BUTTONS

            
On-Camera tactile buttons including all-new record buttons with assignable multifunctional control.
