ESSENTIALS OF FILMMAKING

        
Features & Benefits

        
V-RAPTOR® XE brings the uncompromised image quality of RED’s flagship large format global shutter sensor to a whole new segment of filmmakers. Leveraging the V‑RAPTOR [X] platform, V‑RAPTOR XE includes the essential features for modern filmmaking, including R3D recording at 8K VV 60P, 4K 120P, and 2K 240P.

Dynamic Range

      
With 17+ stops of dynamic range, V-RAPTOR XE delivers both smooth highlight roll-off and unmatched low-light performance.

    

    

      

        
Dynamic Range Uncompromised

        
Where other global shutter cameras compromise dynamic range, V‑RAPTOR XE maintains the impressive 17+ dynamic range and renowned high ISO performance of the original rolling-shutter V‑RAPTOR 8K VV.
